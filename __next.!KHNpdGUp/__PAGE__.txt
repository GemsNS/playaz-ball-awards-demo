/*

 *
 * Each of these three layers covers the whole sky strip, which is two viewports
 * tall — on a 440x850 phone at 3x that is roughly 1320x5100 device pixels each,
 * and animating a layer's opacity keeps all three promoted and recompositing
 * for the life of the page. The organizers reported scrolling still stuttering
 * after the pixel ratio was lowered, and three surfaces that size are among the
 * few things on the page big enough to explain it.
 *
 * Stopping the animation lets them be painted once and left alone. What is lost
 * is the whole field breathing, which is the part the organizers said they
 * could not see anyway; what is kept is the nine sparks, which are a few pixels
 * each and are what actually reads as sparkle. Desktop keeps both.
 */
@media (pointer: coarse){

}

/*
 * The base state of both the sparks and the plane is INVISIBLE, and that is not
 * a detail — it is what makes them safe under reduced motion. The global rule
 * in globals.css collapses every animation to 0.001ms and one iteration, and
 * with the default fill mode an element then reverts to its BASE style. The
 * star layers survive that because their base opacity is 1, which is simply the
 * sky as it was. These two have no business being drawn at all when motion is
 * off: without the opacity:0 below, a visitor who asks for reduced motion gets
 * nine diffraction spikes permanently lit and an airliner parked at the edge.
 * The keyframes start from 0 anyway, so ordinary playback is unchanged.
 */
@keyframes sky-spark{
  0%,84%,100%{opacity:0;transform:scale(.45) rotate(0deg)}
  90%{opacity:1;transform:scale(1) rotate(8deg)}
  94%{opacity:.35;transform:scale(.8) rotate(12deg)}

/*

 *
 * Two layers, one for rain and one for snow, and NEITHER of them animates
 * unless it is actually raining or snowing over Toronto. That is what the
 * [data-precip] attribute on <html> is for: SceneConditionsProbe sets it, and
 * the animation rules below are scoped to it, so on a dry night these are two
 * empty elements at opacity 0 with no animation attached and nothing for the

/*

 * rather than polite.

 * The global rule in globals.css collapses animations to a single 0.001ms
 * iteration, which leaves an element on its BASE style. For the sparks and the
 * aircraft that is opacity 0 and they vanish, which is the whole reason those

 * is set by the rules above, which are not animations, so collapsing the

 * of frozen diagonal scratches. Verified in a capture, not reasoned about.
 *

 * there in the lifted sky, the missing stars and the veiled city, none of which

 * what lets this win without !important.
 */
